Finalbenchmark 2 Pro is a comprehensive CPU benchmarking and performance testing tool, distributed on the Google Play Store.

<b>Key Features:</b>
• Multi-threaded CPU stress testing with real-time monitoring
• Single-core and multi-core performance benchmarks
• Temperature tracking during intensive tests
• AI and GPU workload benchmarks (Vulkan / OpenCL)
• Productivity benchmark with video, image, and document workloads
• Storage and RAM throughput tests
• Detailed per-core frequency, governor, and affinity data
• Historical result tracking and export

<b>Privacy:</b>
Finalbenchmark 2 Pro is fully open source. No tracking, no ads, no telemetry. The app does not request network access for analytics. All benchmark data stays on your device.
